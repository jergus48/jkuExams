Coverage by area

Where JKU is strong

  • Mathematics (31.5 ECTS: linear algebra, analysis, probability, numerical optimization) and statistics. A real edge over bootcamp and self-taught candidates.
  • Classical ML theory: supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement learning, signal processing, NLP basics.
  • Algorithms, data structures, logic, formal models. Useful for interviews and research roles.

Where it leaves you exposed

  • Almost nothing on shipping software: Git workflows, testing, Docker, CI/CD, cloud, APIs, monitoring.
  • Nothing dedicated to LLMs: transformer internals at depth, fine-tuning (LoRA/QLoRA), RAG, agents, evaluation, inference serving.
  • No SQL or data engineering, pipelines, or experiment tracking.
  • About 10.5 ECTS (Responsible AI, Gender Studies, Technology and Society) do not appear in job requirements, apart from EU AI Act awareness being a small plus.

What moves you up the range

  • Production experience: a deployed model serving real users, with monitoring.
  • LLM systems skills (RAG, evaluation, inference optimization) are in current demand.
  • Cloud plus Kubernetes plus MLOps is repeatedly named as the biggest premium.
  • Location: Munich and Zurich pay the most; Berlin the least of the large hubs.
  • An MSc is often preferred for research-heavy and senior roles in Switzerland.
