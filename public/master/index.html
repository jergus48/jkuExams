CTU Prague, Cybernetics and Robotics (KyR)

Master entrance exam prep.

Research pass of 6 Oct 2026 on official CTU FEL pages. The key finding: FEL publishes a real sample test, and it changes what you should study first.

Realistic

Open to other degrees

No specific bachelor is required. Gaps are filled with electives in year one. You must pass a written test and, as a non-KyR graduate, an oral exam or video interview.

Not trivial

JKU AI has no circuits or control

Math, probability, ML and algorithms are strengths. Circuits, op-amps, measurement, control and physics are zero coverage. 17 months is enough if you start now.

The arithmetic of passing

  • You need 9 of 18. Maths and CS are 7 questions, and with timed practice you should get about 6.
  • You then need only 3 more from the 11 electrical, control and measurement questions. That is a realistic target.
  • For a safe 13 of 18 you need about 7 from those 11, which is roughly 65%.
  • Highest yield per hour: measurement and units (Q8, 17, 18), PID concept (Q16), Thevenin (Q10), then op-amps and RLC power.
  • Control with Bode matching (Q13-15) is the longest to learn but also the core of the degree, so start early.
How to use the Study chapters tab: do A and C first (cheap points), then B, then D and E in the final year. Each chapter ends with a practice task that mirrors a sample question. Budget: about 440 hours at 6 h per week over 17 months, and the chapter tags sum to roughly 350 h with the rest for mocks and reviews.

Corrections to earlier answers

  • A sample test does exist. An earlier pass said FEL does not publish one. It is linked from the KyR preparation page.
  • The test is broad bachelor-level multiple choice, not deep master-level theory: maths, code reading, networks, units, circuits, op-amps, control, sensors.
  • The exam is in Czech; non-KyR graduates also face an oral exam or video interview; Austrian degrees need nostrification; the exemption needs a related programme and a 1.80 average.
  • The programme guarantor name and admission email addresses given earlier by another assistant were not verified. Use kyr@fel.cvut.cz.
  • JKU AI module sums given earlier were wrong; use the official curriculum numbers (Data Science 32.5, AI Basics 26, plus 12 ECTS specialization and 9 free electives).
